## Shared terms (all Apixis-family products)
- All paid plans renew monthly automatically.
- Customers pay by card OR with Ixis (100 Ixis = $1).
- Signups via Apixis ID get 1,000 Ixis and an agent sent into Apixis.

## Deduxis (Deduxis + Recovra merged) pricing
- Recovra merges into Deduxis; name stays **Deduxis**. Recovra becomes the "Recover" section; recovra site redirects to Deduxis.
- Receipts: Personal $10/mo (up to 100 receipts); Business $30/mo (unlimited receipts, team, accountant export).
- Recover: free scan, then 25% of money actually recovered (no recovery, no charge).
- Monitor: $100/mo — watches every new bill for overcharges.
- Big companies: contact sales.
- Card or Ixis. Old Recovra Wallet SKUs (recovra.intel.monthly $220, recovra.intel.growth $440) to be retired.
- Update 12:08 AM CT: Monitor subscribers pay 15% (not 25%) on anything recovered.
